## 2. 10-Step Interactive Evaluation Script

### Step 1: Instant Multilingual Voice Interaction (Student AI Tutor)
1. Open the web app at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Ensure you are on the **Student Dashboard** -> **AI Study Companion (RAG)** tab.
3. Switch the language dropdown to **Telugu (`తెలుగు`)** or **Hindi (`हिंदी`)**.
4. Click the **Microphone (Mic)** icon and speak an inquiry, or type:
   - *Telugu:* `"కిరణజన్య సంయోగక్రియ సమీకరణాన్ని వివరించండి"`
   - *English:* `"Explain the formula for constant acceleration with derivation"`
5. Click **Send**.
6. Observe the grounded explanation, and click the **Volume icon (🔊)** to listen to the natural voice response!

### Step 2: AI Smart Examination Simulator (Phase 6.5 Innovation)
1. In Student Dashboard, click the **Exam Simulator (Phase 6.5)** tab.
2. View the pre-seeded **Physics: Kinematics & Newton's Laws Mock Exam**.
3. Click **Start Timed Exam**.
4. Experience the live countdown timer, answer the Multiple Choice, True/False, and Short Answer questions.
5. Click **Submit Exam**.
6. Inspect the instant scorecard with percentage, pass/fail status, and **Topic Mastery Breakdown**!

### Step 3: Smart Notes Scanner & Grounded Doubt Solver
1. In Student Dashboard, click **Notes Scanner & Doubt Solver (Phase 6.5)**.
2. Select the digitized note: *"Kinematics & Acceleration Lecture Notes"*.
3. Click **Review & Correct Text** to see how students verify OCR text and mathematical formulas ($v = u + at$).
4. In the Doubt Solver box, ask: `"What is the formula for velocity under constant acceleration?"`.
5. The AI answers strictly citing the scanned lecture note with confidence score!

### Step 4: AI Smart Timetable & Pomodoro Study Scheduler
1. In Student Dashboard, click **Smart Timetable (Phase 6.5)**.
2. Notice the weekly schedule broken into cognitive Pomodoro focus blocks (45 minutes).
3. Click **Mark Completed (+15 XP)** on any session.
4. An instant notification confirms the session is completed and verified XP is credited!

### Step 5: Gamification & Campus Leaderboard
1. In Student Dashboard, click **Gamification & Badges (Phase 6.5)**.
2. Observe the student's level (**Level 2 • Knowledge Practitioner**), active 4-day streak flame, and XP progress bar.
3. Inspect the verified achievement badges (`FIRST_QUIZ`, `STREAK_7`, `EXAM_SIMULATOR`).
4. Review the opt-in campus leaderboard rankings.

### Step 6: Visual Curriculum Knowledge Map
1. In Student Dashboard, click **Visual Knowledge Map (Phase 6.5)**.
2. View the interactive SVG graph mapping curriculum prerequisites.
3. Observe color-coded mastery: **Mastered** (green), **In Progress** (blue), **Needs Attention** (amber).
4. Click any topic node (e.g. *Linear Equations* or *Calculus Foundations*) to inspect prerequisites and recommended lecture PDFs in the sidebar.

### Step 7: Teacher AI Educator Copilot (Teacher Dashboard)
1. Use the **Quick Role Switcher** to switch to **Teacher**.
2. Go to the **AI Educator Copilot (Phase 6)** tab.
3. Select **Intelligent Lesson Plan Generator**:
   - Subject: *Mathematics* | Topic: *Quadratic Equations* | Duration: *45 min*
4. Click **Generate 11-Section Lesson Plan**.
5. Review the comprehensive pedagogical plan covering prerequisites, active learning activities, differentiation for struggling/advanced learners, and homework.

### Step 8: Early-Warning & Intervention Support
1. In Teacher Dashboard, click **Early-Warning & Interventions (Phase 6.5)**.
2. Review the active risk alerts generated from real telemetry (e.g., student attendance drop or unresolved knowledge gaps).
3. Click **Assign Intervention** to schedule a remedial session.
4. Inspect the **Personalized Supportive Advice for Parents** rendered in English, Telugu, and Hindi!

### Step 9: Parent AI Academic Companion (Parent Dashboard)
1. Switch to **Parent** using the Role Switcher.
2. The **Parent AI Academic Companion** opens automatically.
3. Click one of the quick suggested inquiries:
   - *"How is my child performing in academics overall?"*
   - *"Which areas or subjects need more attention?"*
4. The AI responds grounded strictly in the child's actual database grades and attendance, citing verified records with zero hallucination.
5. Use the microphone button to test voice interaction for parents!

### Step 10: Institutional Admin Intelligence (Admin Dashboard)
1. Switch to **Admin** using the Role Switcher.
2. On the **Overview** tab, review the **Phase 6.5 Innovation Adoption & Learning Metrics** counter.
3. Review the **Institutional Reports** and **RAG Vector Indexing** tabs, showing how syllabus documents are managed across the entire institution.
